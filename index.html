Associate Professor of Economics | University of Cagliari

I hold a PhD in Economics from the University of York.

I am a Research Affiliate at CRENoS and HEDG, a Fellow of the Global Labor Organization (GLO), and affiliated with the Italian Health Economics Association (AIES).

I also serve as Deputy Head of the Department of Economics and Business and as a member of the Comitato Unico di Garanzia (CUG) at the University of Cagliari, which promotes equal opportunities, workplace wellbeing and non-discrimination.

Research interests

My research in health and public economics focuses on health inequalities, on how patients choose and use healthcare services, and on how the organisation of healthcare systems affects health outcomes, including mortality. I am also interested in risky behaviours and the role of public policy in shaping them. More recently, I have become interested in how trust shapes decisions to seek healthcare and in how information about the potential effects of AI on the economy influences support for labour market policies. I also study how workers’ beliefs about the expected benefits and costs of meaningful work shape the value they place on it.
